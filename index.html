Why a flying club

Fly like an owner without owning alone.

Membership gives you owner-like access to the club’s aircraft, a voice in how the club operates, and rates built around sharing fixed costs.

Unlike a traditional rental operation, members support the aircraft together through monthly dues and pay dry hourly rates when they fly. The result is a practical way to stay active, build experience, travel, or train with a club-authorized instructor.

We keep aircraft access meaningful by managing the number of active pilots per airplane and operating under shared standards that protect the members, the aircraft, and the club.

Common questions

What to know before joining.

Is this a flight school?

No. Open Hangar Collective provides aircraft access through membership. Members who need instruction can work with a club-authorized independent instructor and arrange payment directly with that instructor.

What does “dry rate” mean?

The hourly aircraft rate does not include fuel. Members are responsible for fuel under the club’s operating and reimbursement procedures.

How are flights scheduled and billed?

Members schedule aircraft through AirportSync. Each month, flight time is totaled by pilot and aircraft, and a statement is sent by email for payment.

What rules apply to members?

All members must follow the club’s standard operating procedures, including checkout, scheduling, flight limitations, member responsibilities, billing, and aircraft care requirements.

Can non-pilots participate?

People who enjoy general aviation but do not plan to fly may ask about Ground Crew membership and ways to support the club community.
