Nonprofit flying club · IL48 Shafer Field

More access. More flying. More community.

Open Hangar Collective is a nonprofit, non-equity flying club serving the St. Louis Metro East. Members have access to three club aircraft, local aviation connections, social events, and a practical home base for flying and training.

Why join a flying club

Aircraft access without owning alone.

Membership offers a practical way to fly regularly, build experience, travel, or train without carrying the full cost and responsibility of individual aircraft ownership.

The club provides access to three aircraft, a scheduling platform, club-approved independent instructors, and a community of people who enjoy general aviation.

Current membership costs and aircraft rates are provided directly to prospective members. Email us or submit an application when you are ready to learn more.

Around the hangar

Flying is only part of it.

Club life also includes social events, airport projects, aircraft care, safety discussions, and time around the hangar. Facebook and Instagram are the best places for current photos, announcements, and activity.
