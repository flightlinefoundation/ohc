Train, stay proficient, take a local flight, or head somewhere for the weekend without owning an airplane by yourself.

Belong

New to aviation?

You do not have to show up knowing everything.

OHC welcomes pilots at different stages of aviation. If you are still learning, getting back into flying, or simply looking for a place to stay involved, start by talking with us. We will explain the process and what options are currently available.
