The fleet

Different airplanes for different kinds of flying.

From first lessons and proficiency flights to IFR practice and weekend trips, the OHC fleet gives members several ways to stay active in aviation. Tap or click any photo to see a larger view.

N3029X

Cessna 150F

Small, simple, economical, and IFR current

IFR current Two seats Trainer
  • Great for: learning to fly, proficiency flights, local sightseeing, and affordable time building
  • Easy to get comfortable in and a familiar platform for primary training
  • A practical choice for members who want a straightforward airplane for short hops and skill work

N7892W

Piper Cherokee PA-28-180

IFR-current four-seat Cherokee with a GNS 430W

IFR current GNS 430W 180 HP
  • Great for: instrument flying, cross-country trips, staying current, and carrying a little more airplane with you
  • Garmin GNS 430W IFR GPS navigator for capable IFR travel and practice
  • A solid step up for members ready for a four-seat platform with more mission flexibility

N5389W

Piper Cherokee PA-28-160

Classic four-seat Cherokee for training, travel, and practical cross-country flying

Four seats Training S-TEC autopilot
  • Great for: training, local flights, weekend trips, and getting comfortable in a four-seat Cherokee
  • Equipped with an S-TEC autopilot for added utility on cross-country and proficiency flying
  • A useful step-up airplane for members building confidence beyond a two-seat trainer
