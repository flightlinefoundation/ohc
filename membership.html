Membership

A straightforward path to the flight line.

Qualified members can schedule club aircraft through AirportSync after completing the onboarding and checkout requirements that apply to them.

The club does not sell flight instruction. Members may train with club-approved independent instructors.

Current membership costs and aircraft rates are provided directly when you inquire or apply.

How to join

From interest to aircraft access.

01

Submit the membership application

Tell us about yourself, your aviation experience, and the kind of flying you hope to do.

02

Review club materials

We privately provide the welcome packet, aircraft references, operating guidelines, rate information, and club documents needed for onboarding.

03

Meet the club and airplanes

Visit Shafer Field for an airport walkthrough, orientation, and a look at the aircraft and hangar procedures.

04

Arrange your checkout

Rated pilots complete a checkout with a club-approved independent CFI. Student pilots complete the requirements applicable to their training and medical status.

05

Complete the aircraft and hangar review

Before the checkout flight, we review preflight procedures, hangar operations, and club-specific items you need to know.

06

Receive scheduling access

After the applicable checkout requirements are complete and documented, we activate your AirportSync account.

Common questions

What to know before joining.

Is this a flight school?

No. Open Hangar Collective provides aircraft access through membership. Members who need instruction can work with a club-approved independent instructor.

How are flights scheduled and billed?

Members schedule aircraft through AirportSync. Flight time and other applicable charges are billed under the club’s current procedures.

What rules apply to members?

Members must comply with all applicable FAA requirements and other federal, state, and local laws and regulations, as well as the club bylaws, operating rules, checkout requirements, scheduling procedures, flight limitations, billing procedures, and aircraft-care requirements.

Can non-pilots participate?

People who enjoy general aviation but do not plan to fly may ask about Ground Crew membership and other ways to participate in the club community.
